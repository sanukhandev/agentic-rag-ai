AJMAN NUVENTURES CENTRE

FREE ZONE (ANCFZ)

STRATEGIC PARTNERS

ANCFZ works with banking and payment partners to support businesses with corporate bank account opening and payment setup. Bank account approval is subject to the selected bank’s eligibility criteria, documentation requirements, and compliance checks. Opening a company in ANCFZ does not guarantee bank account approval.

Partner Categories

1. Easy Payment Solutions 
This category covers payment collection for licensed companies, from card acceptance to online checkout, which is the next step after the bank account is open.
