AJMAN NUVENTURES CENTRE

FREE ZONE (ANCFZ)

TYPES OF COMPANIES

Start Your Business

1. Free Zone Establishment (FZE)
An FZE license is under the category of a single shareholder will enjoy the status of a totally independent corporate entity.

Required Documents

Passport copy

Colored photograph

Residence visa and Emirates ID copy (if resident)

Tourist visa / Entry stamp (if on a tourist visa)


2. Free Zone Company (FZC)
An FZC is a business entity that can have two or more shareholders, with no stated maximum shareholder limit.

Required Documents

Passport copy (all shareholders)

Coloured photograph (all shareholders)

Residence visa and Emirates ID copy (if resident) (all shareholders)

Tourist visa / Entry stamp (if on a tourist visa) (all shareholders)


3. Branch of Foreign Company 
Companies based outside of the United Arab Emirates are granted licenses under this category. The corporation will have 100% foreign ownership.

Required Documents

Passport copy of the Company Manager

Coloured photograph of the Company Manager

Residence visa and Emirates ID copy of the Manager (if resident)

Tourist visa / Entry stamp of the Manager (if on a tourist visa)

Trade License copy (attested)

MOA copy (attested)

Board Resolution copy (attested)

Foreign company documents must be attested by the Foreign Office and UAE Embassy in the country of issue, followed by Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation in the UAE.

4. Branch of Local Company 
A company holding a valid license from any other UAE licensing authority is eligible for this license.

Required Documents

Passport copy of the company manager

Coloured photograph of the company manager

Residence visa and Emirates ID copy of the manager (if resident)

Tourist visa / Entry stamp of the manager (if on a tourist visa)

Trade License copy (notarized and attested)

MOA copy / AOA copy (notarized and attested)

Board Resolution copy (notarized and attested)

UAE company documents must be notarized by a Notary Public in the UAE or attested by the license-issuing authority in the UAE.
