Frequently Asked Questions


1. How much does an ANCFZ license cost?

An ANCFZ all-inclusive package starts at AED 4,888 per year for a zero-visa company and goes up to AED 52,200 per year for a 10-visa package. Multi-year packages from 2 to 5 years are also listed.


2. What is included in the ANCFZ package price?

Every package includes the business license, lease agreement, commercial registry, share certificate, certificate of incorporation, certificate of incumbency, a bank letter to support the account opening process, unlimited shareholders and up to 10 mix-and-match activities. Visa packages also cover the listed visa-related services and Emirates ID processing. Bank account approval is subject to the selected bank’s eligibility criteria, documentation requirements and compliance checks, and is not guaranteed.


3. Are there any costs not included?

Health insurance is mandatory during the visa process and is not included in the package price.


4. Can I pay for multiple years upfront?

Yes. Multi-year packages are available for 2 to 5 years. The 1-year full-advance price includes a 10% upfront payment discount. Renewal follows the approved price list for the chosen duration, and downgrading at renewal is permitted based on visas utilized.


5. Do I need to visit the UAE to set up my company?

Company registration can be completed remotely, with license issuance in as little as 2 hours, provided all required documents are in order. A visit may be required for visa-related procedures, including the medical fitness test and Emirates ID.
