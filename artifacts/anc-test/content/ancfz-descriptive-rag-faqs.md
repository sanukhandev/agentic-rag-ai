AJMAN NUVENTURES CENTRE

FREE ZONE (ANCFZ)

Introduction to ANCFZ

1. What is the Ajman NuVentures Centre Free Zone (ANCFZ)?
ANCFZ is a new-generation free zone in Ajman, UAE, designed to support startups, SMEs, and international investors. It offers fast-track company setup, competitive packages, and a wide range of business activities.

2. Who can set up a company in ANCFZ?
Any individual, entrepreneur, or corporate investor (local or international) can establish a company in ANCFZ. There are no nationality restrictions for shareholders. The low-cost Ajman company setup makes it easier for stakeholders to join. ANCFZ also offers the Cheapest Free Zone in the UAE for Company Set up.

3. Where is ANCFZ located?
ANCFZ is based in Ajman, UAE, and is strategically located close to Ajman Port, Sharjah International Airport, and Dubai International Airport.

Company Setup and Structure
4. How many business activities are available at ANCFZ?
ANCFZ offers over 3,500 licensed business activities, organized into categories. Up to 10 activities can be combined under one license.

5. What types of company structures are available?
The available company structures are:

Free Zone Establishment (FZE): For a single shareholder (individual or corporate).

Free Zone Company (FZC): For multiple shareholders, including the Free Zone Company with Visa Dubai.

Branch Office: A branch of an existing local or foreign company.


6. What is the minimum share capital requirement?
ANCFZ has no minimum paid-up capital requirement for most business activities.

7. How long does the company registration take?
Company registration and licence issuance can be completed in as little as 2 hours, provided all required documents are in order.

8. What documents are required for company registration?

Passport copy of shareholders and directors

Passport-size photographs

Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or tenancy contract)

Application form and proposed company name(s)

Note: Additional documents may be required depending on the activity and shareholder type (individual or corporate).

Benefits and Regulations

9. What are the benefits of setting up in ANCFZ?

100% foreign ownership

No personal income tax

No capital gains tax

No property tax

Competitive setup costs and packages

Wide range of business activities under one license

Strategic location in the UAE with global connectivity

ANCFZ also offers a Blockchain Tokenization License Dubai, an E-Commerce License UAE, online company registration in the UAE, and a Free Zone Company University License.

10. Are there restrictions on business activities?
Most commercial, service, and consultancy activities are permitted. However, regulated activities such as financial services, healthcare, and education may require additional approvals from relevant authorities.

11. How can I renew my license?
Licenses must be renewed annually. The process is straightforward and can often be completed online without the need for shareholders to be physically present.

Visas and Corporate Services

12. Does ANCFZ offer visa issuance?
ANCFZ provides fast visa issuance within 24–48 hours after company registration.

Investor/Partner visas (for shareholders)

Employment visas (for staff)

The process includes a medical fitness test, Emirates ID, and visa stamping.

13. Can companies hire employees under ANCFZ?
Yes, licensed companies are entitled to apply for UAE residence visas for their employees, shareholders, and dependents. The visa quota depends on the office space/package chosen.

14. Is physical office space mandatory?
ANCFZ offers flexi-desk, shared office, and private office options. For most businesses, flexi-desk solutions are sufficient and cost-effective. Physical office space is only required for specific activities.

15. Can I open a corporate bank account with an ANCFZ license?
ANCFZ supports companies with corporate bank account opening through its banking partners. Approval is subject to the selected bank’s eligibility criteria, documentation requirements, and compliance checks. Opening a company in ANCFZ does not guarantee bank account approval.
