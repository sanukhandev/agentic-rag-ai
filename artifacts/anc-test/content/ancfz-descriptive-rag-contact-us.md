AJMAN NUVENTURES CENTRE

FREE ZONE (ANCFZ)

Contact Us


Expert guidance just a call away

Ajman NuVentures Centre Free Zone (ANCFZ) is a licensing authority based in Ajman, with a representative office in Dubai. The team can answer questions about licence types and costs, visa allocations, renewals, bank account opening and moving an existing company into the free zone.


Contact the authority directly by phone or email. Investors based in Dubai can also contact the representative office for in-person meetings.


Which Office Should You Contact?

Head Office in Ajman: licensing, visas, renewals and amendments to an existing license.
 Amber Gem Tower, Mezzanine Floor, Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Street, Al Rashidiya 3, Ajman, P.O. Box 4848. 
Phone: [REDACTED_PHONE]. 
Email: [REDACTED_EMAIL]

Dubai representative office: meetings for investors based in Dubai and the southern emirates. Office 901, 9th Floor, Aspin Commercial Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai. 
Phone: [REDACTED_PHONE].
 Email: [REDACTED_EMAIL]


What to Have Ready

To help the team provide a package quote and timeline, prepare the following details:

Business activities you plan to run

Number of residence visas required

Number of shareholders and their nationalities

Office requirement: physical office or flexi-desk








Frequently Asked Questions

How long does registration take?

About two hours once all documents are in order.


What documents do I need?

Passport copies of shareholders and directors, passport photographs, proof of residence such as a utility bill or tenancy contract, and the application form with proposed company names.


Is there a minimum share capital?

There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement for most activities.


How fast are visas?

Visa processing is estimated to take 24–48 hours. The timeline may vary depending on the required procedures, including the medical fitness test, Emirates ID and visa stamping.


Do I need an office?

No. Flexi-desk, shared office and private office options are available.


Can I register without coming to the UAE?

Yes, when all documents are complete. Travel is needed at the visa stage for the medical test and Emirates ID.



Existing Customers

Licence holders and channel partners can use the ANCFZ portals for renewals, visa applications and document requests. The customer portal and agent portal are linked in the Portals menu on the website.

For longer answers, refer to the FAQ. For current package prices, refer to the Packages and Offers page.
