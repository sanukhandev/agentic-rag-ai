## Result

**VERIFIED for the private v2 evidence boundary.** `verifyV2ResponseEvidence`
parses the strict persona response, validates citation membership and source
status, checks approved/current evidence semantics, supports explicitly
verified structured facts, detects conflicting numeric evidence, records
redacted claim digests and returns a typed response-guard verdict.

Citation IDs are not treated as semantic proof: claim terms and numbers must
also be supported by the cited evidence or verified structured fact. Revoked or
unapproved sources, unknown citations, unsupported consequential claims,
conflicting sources, metadata leakage and instruction-injection output are
rejected. Uncited content is accepted only for explicitly marked, safe
conversational wording. The verifier does not authorize actions or provider
state transitions.
