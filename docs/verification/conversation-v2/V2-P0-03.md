## Frozen safety decisions

- `schemaVersion: 2` is mandatory in both the manifest and `CatalogManifest`
  row; v1/unversioned documents fail closed.
- Unknown executable sheets or row fields fail closed instead of being dropped.
- Stage requirements are scoped to the owning goal and stage; stage order must
  be contiguous from 1 and later-stage documents cannot silently block Stage 1.
- Tool keys are catalog data, not authorization. Read-only tools have no
  workflow, consent or confirmation; proposals/external writes require an
  approved registry binding, purpose-specific consent and explicit customer
  confirmation.
- Catalog identity is deterministic and immutable from canonical JSON SHA-256.
- Legacy conversion produces a hash-bound `review_required` candidate with
  `autoPublish: false`; it never carries consent, actions or publication state.
