### Locked invariants

- Stage order is exactly Lead Generation → Lead Qualification → Account
  Creation → Package Selection → Document Upload → Payment & Invoice →
  Contract.
- A confirmed goal is not action consent. Each side effect requires its own
  published gate, identity binding, idempotency and verified provider evidence.
- n8n execution, HTTP success, local IDs, generated PDFs and `mock_paid` never
  establish remote completion by themselves.
- No card or bank credentials are collected in chat.
- Legal registration is `separately_tracked`; it is not implied by a verified
  contract stage.
- Missing owner rules remain blocked inputs. They are not invented by GPT or
  generic Node code.
